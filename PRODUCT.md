# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The owner, for personal use: signing contracts, filling in forms and touching up PDFs day to day on a Mac.

## Product Purpose

A desktop PDF toolkit (Tauri) to sign, annotate, fill, reorganize, merge, split, compress and protect PDFs. Success: an everyday PDF chore takes a few clicks and no account.

## Positioning

Local, fast and free, the opposite of Stirling PDF (now paid and clunky) and Acrobat: files never leave the machine, the app opens instantly, no feature sits behind a paywall.

## Operating Context

macOS first (Tauri webview on WebKit); it must still work on Windows and Linux without special care. Single window, single user, files opened from Finder or dragged in. Interface language: Italian.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Live editor over PDF.js-rendered pages: text, date, check/cross marks, drawn or uploaded signatures, white-out, highlight, true redaction, AcroForm filling. Pages view: reorder, rotate, delete, extract, split, merge PDFs, images to PDF, pages to PNG. Page numbers and watermark stamps. Save with flatten, JPEG compression, password and metadata. Export goes through pdf-lib (@cantoo fork) with standard Helvetica, so only WinAnsi characters are supported.

## Brand Commitments

The owner asked for a Figma-like editor: floating toolbars and panels over the canvas.

## Evidence on Hand

No logo, brand assets or users beyond the owner.

## Product Principles

- Nothing leaves the computer.
- The common job (open, sign, save) is the shortest path.
- What you see on the page is exactly what gets saved.
